How to Make a Simple Resume

Organize experience, education and skills into a readable resume.

A readable resume helps employers quickly understand your experience, skills and qualifications.

Use Resume Builder to enter your name, contact details, target role, summary, experience, education and skills. Download the PDF and proofread names, dates, spelling and contact details.

Use clear headings and achievement-focused bullet points. Do not include sensitive personal details that are not needed for the application.
