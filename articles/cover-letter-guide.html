How to Write a Cover Letter

A practical structure for a concise, tailored job application letter.

A cover letter should explain why your experience fits the role and why you are interested in the employer.

Open Cover Letter Builder, enter the employer and job title, then write a concise, tailored message. Mention one or two relevant examples and end with a professional closing.

Always verify the hiring manager’s name and company details. The builder provides a simple document layout; it does not independently verify facts or guarantee a job outcome.
