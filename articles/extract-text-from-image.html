How to Extract Text from an Image

Use OCR to copy text from photos and screenshots.

Optical character recognition (OCR) attempts to turn pixels in an image into editable text.

Open Image to Text (OCR), choose a clear image and select its language. Start with a high-resolution, well-lit image that is straight and has strong contrast. Check the extracted result carefully before copying or downloading it.

OCR is not perfect. Handwriting, ornate fonts, blurred photos and mixed languages can reduce accuracy. Urdu Nastaliq and right-to-left scripts may need especially careful proofreading.
